Privacy policy

Health Director is a personal tool used by a single person (its owner). It is not offered to anyone else.

Google data accessed

  • Calendar (read-only): event times and titles, to find free time for health activities.
  • Calendars created by the app: the app creates one calendar (“Здоровье”) and adds, moves or removes only its own events there.

The app never edits, deletes or shares the owner's other calendar events.

Storage and sharing

Event times and titles are stored in the owner's private database to build a daily plan. Data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Google user data is handled in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
